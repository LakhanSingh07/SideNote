Privacy draft

Notes and attached files are stored on your devices. Local network sync transfers them to PCs you pair. Trial verification sends a SideNote-specific hashed Windows installation identifier, app device ID and verification times; it does not send the raw Windows installation identifier, note bodies or file contents. Purchase verification uses subscription references, activation credentials and linked device IDs. Razorpay processes payment information. Operator identity, contact address, retention periods and deletion process must be completed by the owner before publication.
