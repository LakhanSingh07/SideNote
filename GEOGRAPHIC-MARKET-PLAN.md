# SideNote country targeting and pricing

Research date: 16 October 2026. Owner clarification: both apps are useful standalone. Windows users do not need Android; Android users do not need Windows. Sync and file transfer are additional benefits. Android is free during the initial beta, so Android installs and Windows paid customers must be measured separately.

## What competitor country data can establish

No verified country-level paid-customer or revenue breakdown was found for Notezilla, UpNote, Stickies.lol or Obsidian in the public sources checked. Website traffic is not paying customers, app installs, or active app users. Company location, hosting country, review language and developer nationality are not customer geography.

- [Semrush/Exploding Topics Obsidian report](https://analytics.explodingtopics.com/website/obsidian.md): an indexed August 2026 report showed US 20.87%, Russia 7.24%, Brazil 5.29%, Japan 5.28%, Germany 5.21% of estimated website traffic. Opening the report returned a September 2026 page whose detailed geography was still loading. These figures cannot be presented as a current verified customer split.
- [HypeStat Obsidian report](https://hypestat.com/info/obsidian.md): search and opened snapshots disagreed materially about shares and leading countries; the opened report contained month labels without a usable consistent year. Exclude precise shares from a current market ranking. The US appears as a substantial market in the observed estimates, but claims that it is definitely the largest paying market are unsupported.
- [HypeStat UpNote help-site report](https://hypestat.com/info/help.getupnote.com): its estimate lists South Korea, Japan and Taiwan as leading countries. This is a documentation subdomain with an unclear measurement period, not UpNote's complete customer base. It is at most a weak signal for later localization research.
- Notezilla and Stickies.lol: no dependable public country breakdown was retrieved. Conceptworld.com also contains other products, so whole-domain traffic would not isolate Notezilla sales.

This evidence does not support a definitive claim about where competitors get most paying customers. It supports a small geographic validation plan, not a sales forecast.

## Platform fit: standalone Windows matters

[Statcounter desktop estimates](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/desktop/india/) for September 2026 show Windows at 82.31% in India; [Germany](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/desktop/germany/) 76.60%; [US](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/desktop/united-states-of-america/) 64.45%. These measure sampled desktop web usage, not people who own a PC, software purchase rates, or market size. They should not be multiplied by Android shares to invent a joint audience.

Android mobile web-use estimates for the same month were [India 93.89%](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/india/), [Germany 72.37%](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/germany/), [US 46.39%](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/united-states-of-america/). These are relevant only to the optional Android companion opportunity. US Windows+iPhone users remain eligible for standalone Windows SideNote.

## Recommended markets to test

1. **India: early user recruitment and a regional-price paid test.** Existing app distribution and familiarity may reduce the owner's recruiting/support friction, but the actual geography of the owner's existing apps is unknown. Target Windows users doing repeated study/work tasks; do not equate low-cost installs with revenue. Freelancers and junior professionals are a useful paid hypothesis; students are a separate price-sensitive hypothesis to test.
2. **United States: parallel English-language paid-demand test.** Test standalone Windows capture and visual task management first, with Android sync as an additional benefit. Competitor website estimates provide a directional interest signal, not a guarantee of stronger willingness to pay or lower acquisition costs. Qualify visitors for Windows compatibility; do not require Android.
3. **Germany: a subsequent localized test.** Windows usage provides product-fit evidence. German landing copy, understandable local pricing, appropriate support and checkout readiness need validation before spending broadly. Do not claim this is a proven top-paying country.
4. **UK/Canada/Australia: expansion candidates for English material**, after results from initial tests. No competitor paid-country data was retrieved to rank these against each other.
5. **Japan/South Korea/Taiwan: later research/localization candidates.** UpNote help traffic alone is too weak to justify immediate paid acquisition or choosing these as the primary market.

These are test priorities based on fit and execution effort, not a prediction that one country will certainly supply the most customers.

## Pricing implications of standalone value

Direct official benchmarks:
- [Stickies.lol](https://stickies.lol/#pricing): USD 4.99 one-time launch offer (USD 14.99 crossed out), three personal Windows PCs, seven-day trial. No built-in device sync according to its FAQ. This is the strongest pressure on pricing for standalone screen-edge notes; the temporary launch price is not evidence of long-term market-wide willingness to pay.
- [Notezilla](https://www.conceptworld.com/Notezilla/BuyNow): USD 19.95/year plus USD 10 in the first year; USD 29.95 one-time alternative with only the first year of sync. The desktop license continues under its published terms.
- [UpNote](https://getupnote.com/#pricing): USD 1.99/month or USD 39.99 lifetime; regional pricing/taxes may differ. Its broader note management and sync should be considered when evaluating SideNote's scope.

Matching a competitor's number without matching billing duration, included devices, feature maturity and sync scope would be misleading. A seven-day trial does not mean a one-time USD 4.99 competitor validates a USD 4.99 monthly subscription.

For the owner's current monthly/yearly Windows model, retain these as **unapproved experiments**: India INR 79/month or INR 599/year; international USD 1.99/month or USD 14.99/year. USD is an initial international pricing proposal, not automatically available local currency for every country. A Windows+iPhone customer receives the complete standalone value and must not be told they need Android to justify purchase. Show how additional sync works for users who choose the companion.

Because standalone desktop value is central, explicitly test recurring-payment resistance. If active standalone users reject subscription and request ownership, evaluate a one-time license for a defined desktop version as a separate offer before building more payment complexity. That would change the chosen model and needs an explicit business decision; no one-time plan has been implemented or created. Do not promise lifetime hosted services to sell a local app.

No Razorpay price or country-specific plan was created. The current backend has one configured monthly/yearly plan pair, not an implemented regional price catalog. Adding multiple regional currencies requires server-approved plan IDs and checkout selection before advertising them. An active Razorpay account does not establish international recurring checkout readiness: confirm the relevant methods/currencies in the account and verify a real test transaction. See [Razorpay Subscriptions](https://razorpay.com/subscriptions/) and [international payments](https://razorpay.com/international/).

## How to choose using our own evidence

Run distinct campaigns for standalone Windows, standalone Android beta, and the two-device workflow. Initially compare India and US Windows visitors with the same core task and compatible hardware requirements. Treat Germany as the next localized experiment. Use searchable demonstrations such as 'sticky notes for Windows', 'desktop reminder notes', and a separate 'Android Windows notes sync' workflow rather than claiming unverified keyword volumes or ad costs.

Record source/campaign, voluntarily provided or aggregate country, eligible Windows visitors, downloads, trial starts, successful first note, day-7 return, paid conversion, cancellations/refunds and actual spend. Do not collect note contents or files for marketing analytics. Android installs are not Windows paid conversions. Enable analytics only with appropriate disclosure/consent rather than silently adding it.

Compare net contribution after actual acquisition/support/payment costs and refunds; conversions alone do not determine the best market. Do not predict acquisition costs from country stereotypes. Small pilot results are directional, and paid transactions are stronger evidence than compliments or raw downloads. Do not launch paid campaigns or create live plans without the owner's concrete budget/pricing decision.
